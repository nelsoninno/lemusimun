Frequently asked questions

What does Lemusimún do?

Lemusimún is an advertising agency in El Salvador with 50 years of experience. We create strategy, creativity, media, audiovisual production and data for brands that want to be impossible to ignore.

Where is Lemusimún located?

We are in Antiguo Cuscatlán, El Salvador (Res. Arcos de Santa Elena, Calle Apaneca #4C). We serve brands across the country.

What advertising and marketing services do you offer?

360 strategies, media strategy and buying, digital media, audiovisual production, events and BTL, brand analysis and data, business intelligence and public relations.

How many years of experience does the agency have?

Founded in 1976, Lemusimún has 50 years, three generations and more than 1000 campaigns for over 300 brands.

Do you work in Spanish and English?

Yes. We serve clients in Spanish and English, with local and international creative thinking thanks to our alliance with The Collective.

How can I contact Lemusimún?

Email us at info@lemusimuncorp.com or call +(503) 2233-5000. You can also find us on Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.
